Privacy policy

Word Wizard · updated October 12, 2026 · Español · Português

Word Wizard is a word game by CapraLabsGames. This page explains what data the game uses, on the web or in the Android app, and why.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, achievements, quests and settings are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage. They are not sent anywhere.

What is sent for the world ranking

When you finish a daily challenge or a climb up the Tower, the game sends your result to the world ranking:

  • the wizard name you chose (it is public: anyone can see it in the ranking);
  • the date, the challenge number, the points, the number of words and the rank; for the Tower, your best score and floor;
  • an anonymous identifier created automatically the first time, so each wizard counts once per day. It is not linked to your email, phone or any account of yours.

You don't need to sign up. If you'd rather not appear with your real name, pick a wizard name that isn't. Offensive names are replaced with a generic one.

The data is stored with Supabase (servers in São Paulo, Brazil), our database provider. It is kept while the wizard exists; it is never sold or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your email, phone or a password.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, or third-party cookies.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Feedback, words and errors

To fix the game we receive three things, never with your wizard name or any personal data:

  • Feedback: if you tap "What do you think?" and send a comment, we receive your text, the face you picked and how you've been playing (level, days played, minutes per day and best Tower floor). You can also send it through WhatsApp: in that case it's only sent if you send it.
  • Missing words: if you tap "Is it a word?" on a word the game didn't accept, we receive that word and the challenge word. We review them, but not all of them get added.
  • Errors: if something breaks, the game sends only the technical error message.

Each of them includes the game version, the language and the type of device (system, browser, whether it's installed and the screen size). It's stored in Supabase, the same provider as the ranking, only to improve the game, and deleted after 12 months. It's not used for ads or shared.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the reminder in Settings, we store the notification address your browser or phone provides, your time zone, the time you chose, the language and the last day you played the challenge (so we don't remind you if you already played). Nothing else: not your name or number. Turn it off in Settings and it is deleted.

Purchases (Wizard Pass and outfits)

You can only buy in the Android app, and payment is handled by Google Play: your payment details (card, account) stay with Google and never reach us. To confirm each purchase we receive from Google the product, the order number, its status (active, canceled, expired) and the expiry date, together with your wizard's anonymous identifier if there is one. It is stored in Supabase to acknowledge the purchase, handle support requests and keep accounts, and kept as long as accounting rules require. It is not used for ads and is not shared.

Deleting your data

In Settings → "Delete this wizard" the wizard is deleted from your device along with its world ranking results. If you no longer have the game installed, or you'd rather ask us, email soporte@capralabsgames.com with your wizard name and we'll delete it within 30 days.

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data (Argentine Personal Data Protection Law 25,326). The Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP) is the supervisory authority for that law.

Children

The game doesn't ask for personal data. If you are a minor, use a wizard name that isn't your real name.

Changes

If we add something that uses other data (for example, signing in with Google to save your progress), we'll announce it in the game and update this page before turning it on.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to soporte@capralabsgames.com. Word Wizard is a game by CapraLabsGames. See also the terms and conditions.
